What gets recorded

  • The text of your Google searches
  • The date and time of each search
  • A category we work out from the search text

What never gets recorded

  • Page contents, passwords or anything you type into forms
  • Any website other than Google search
  • Anything sent to a server — there is no server

You can pause tracking or delete everything at any time.
